    Model II · Sections 5 and 7.3

    Optimal search separation

    Find the global minimum expected time to discover food and approach it together.

    H(d) = 1 / [k(A(d) + B(d))] + (L + αd) / v

    A(d) is the area covered by two detection disks. B(d) is zero through 2L and equals β[1 − exp(−γ(d − 2L))] beyond 2L.
